### Handbrot

its a regular bread.

1. roll out into a flat rectangle (like strudle)
2. then fill the center long edge
3. fold over each edge and seal each ends
4. flip the loaf so the seam is on the bottom
5. press a spoon handel down to create a bunch of loafs
6. score the top and bake

### Apfelmus Hornchen

Its a regular topfen bletter teig, but 
- 150g butter & 100g apple mousse
- no eggs

Then when you roll the croissants out into triangles, you spread apple mousse between the layers.
This stops the layers from sticking together.

bake at 160c for 20min.
