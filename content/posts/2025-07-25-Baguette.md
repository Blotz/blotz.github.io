This is a work-in-progress recipe that I'm still developing while working in the Alps,
I still need to figure out the baking process without a Dutch oven,
I'll update this recipe as I refine and perfect it.

## Ingredients

- 500g flour
- 70% lukewarm water
- 2% salt
- 8-10g fresh yeast (conversion table [here](https://www.dovesfarm.co.uk/hints-&-tips/fresh-to-dry-yeast-conversion-table))[^1]

I also add a teaspoon of honey (for the malt).

[^1]: I haven't quite figured out the proofing times yet,
It depends on how late in the evening you start your bread.

## Preparation

I'm quite confident with this section.

Start preparing the baguette the evening before.
It takes about 2 hours to prep, so 6-7 PM works well.

### Autolyse/Proof

Mix all your ingredients in a wooden bowl until all the flour is combined,
It should look like a shaggy, messy dough, Now, cover the bowl with cling film and let it rest for 30 minutes.

Every 30 minutes, you need to perform a [Stretch and Fold](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HprDjPWuiN8), With *wet* hands[^2], pull and work the dough,
Then, let it rest again,
Do this 3 or 4 times until the dough is smooth and well-developed.

[^2]: Wet hands will prevent the dough from sticking to your hands.

Now, slow down the proofing by putting the dough in the fridge overnight.[^3]

[^3]: While not necessary, you can shape your dough into a loaf which makes it easier to handle the next day.

## Shape and Bake

In the morning, divide the dough into two parts and begin shaping.
Try to maintain as much tension in the skin of the baguette as possible.
> I recommend following this [guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRDL3lPQSkc) if you are unsure how to shape a baguette.
Once shaped, leave the bread to rise and immediately preheat the oven,
The baguette takes roughly 30 minutes to rise, so it's important to start immediately.

Right before baking the baguettes, score the tops. 
Scores should be longer and deep to give the baguette plenty of room to rise.

I'm still working out the baking process,
My current best theory is:

Preheat the oven to 250°C (480°F) with a metal tray[^4] on the bottom rack.

Immediately after placing the baguette in the oven, quickly pour a cup of boiling water into the hot oven and close the door.
It is **critical** that you don't open the oven door and release the steam.

Bake the baguette at 250°C (480°F) for 10 minutes,
Then bake at 230°C (450°F) for 5 minutes[^5].

[^5]: I'm not completely sure about this time,
Keep checking until it has just the right color,
I personally bake until I can smell the baguette.
