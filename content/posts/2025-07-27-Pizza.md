My go-to recipe for a perfect pizza base.

This recipe is a slow fermentation recipe which is supposed to take 2-3 days.
The goal is to bring as much flavor into the dough and slowly work it into the ideal condition.
It is really important that you have a *super* hot oven when baking this pizza. 

Makes 3 pizza bases

## Ingredients

- 540g Quality Strong Flour[^2]
- 80% cold water
- 2% salt
- 5g Fresh yeast (conversion table [here](https://www.dovesfarm.co.uk/hints-&-tips/fresh-to-dry-yeast-conversion-table))[^1]
- Olive Oil

[^1]: the amount of yeast you put in will determine how long the proof is for.
5g will be finished in exactly 2 days. Less will take longer.

[^2]: Better flour has more protine in which will result in a stronger more glutinous dough.
If you want to go the extra mile, get some 00 pizza flour.

## Autolyse/Proof

While making this dough, its important to be super aware of the temperature.
This dough should always remain in the fridge when not being handled.

In a small bowl, add your water and yeast. 
Then slowly whisk till all the yeast has dissolved into the water.
In a large bowl, combine your flour, salt, and yeast-water.
Cover the large bowl with clingfilm and let it rest in the fridge.


After 30min to an hour, you need to perform a [Stretch and Fold](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HprDjPWuiN8).
With *wet* hands[^3], pull and work the dough.
Then, let it rest again.
You should do this every hour or so, returning it to the fridge when done.

You should do this for 48 hours.

[^3]: Wet hands will prevent the dough from sticking to your hands.

The pizza will be ready to bake once it's doubled in size.

## Bake

When baking your pizza, make sure to get the oven as hot as possible.
And remember, less is more when it comes to toppings. 
If you add too much sauce or toppings, the pizza won't bake properly, and you will get a soggy base.
